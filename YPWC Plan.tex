\section*{Overview}
This document is a practical plan for running a physics writing competition at
our school. The aim is to push students to turn physics into writing people
actually enjoy reading, in a magazine style rather than a formal paper.

\section*{Suitable Topics}
\begin{itemize}
\item Space and Astronomy
\item Electricity and Magnetism
\item Waves and Sound
\item Modern Physics
\item Quantum Physics (simplified)
\item Thermodynamics
\item Physics in Daily Life
\end{itemize}

\section*{Format}
Each article follows a magazine structure:
\begin{itemize}
\item Title
\item Introduction: a short hook that catches attention
\item Main Explanation: the physics idea made clear
\item Real-life Applications
\item Interesting Insight: a surprising fact or paradox
\item Conclusion: a brief closing
\end{itemize}

Length sits between 600 and 1500 words. Write for a curious nonspecialist. Keep
language warm and clear. Graphs, figures, and GIFs are welcome. Analogies and
everyday examples help. AI-generated text is not accepted.

\section*{Submission and Dates}
Articles go in as Word or PDF, alongside an optional image or GIF. The form
collects name, school, and topic. Registration is open from September 4 to
September 30. Judging runs from October 1 to October 5.

\section*{Evaluation}
Each piece is scored out of 100:
\begin{itemize}
\item Scientific accuracy - 40
\item Clarity and flow - 25
\item Creativity and originality - 20
\item Real-life connection - 10
\item References and support - 5
\end{itemize}

Mentors and editors read each article independently against the same rubric so
the process stays fair.

\section*{Results and Awards}
There is a single round. The top 30\% of participants receive medals in a
Gold : Silver : Bronze ratio of 3 : 2 : 1. The top three on the leaderboard get
special prizes, and everyone receives a certificate. Results are announced on
October 5.

\section*{Publication}
The strongest articles are gathered into a digital magazine issue for the wider
school community to read. The finished magazine is the real output of the
competition, not just a ranking.
